A distance rate prices a job by distance: a base charge, a distance included in it, and a charge per distance unit beyond that, each with a driver-pay equivalent. It needs: name, speed, at least one vehicle size, base charge, base charge (driver), per distance unit, per distance unit (driver) and distance included. Depots are optional and tie the rate to jobs from those depots.

Customer codes are optional: blank means every customer.

## Distance unit

Kilometres or miles according to the tenant's country setting; the rate doesn't choose.

## Depots

Pick from the tenant's depots. If none are listed, DFRNT hasn't created them yet (Business step checklist).

## Driver fields

Required because Settings requires them. Enter 0 where drivers are paid another way.
