A partner is another carrier or agent you hand work to. It needs only a name. Give it a contact email if the partner should sign in to their own portal: Settings then creates the partner's client file, its primary contact and a login, and emails them an invite, so the row also needs the contact's role and the "send sign-in invite" tick.

Driver pay % (what the partner is paid of the job) must be between 0 and 100 if given. "Network partner" means the partner has its own client file and portal; untick it for a plain agent.

## Optional details

Contact name, phone, address, city, state and post code.

## A brand-new tenant needs a customer first

Settings copies a few site and billing values for a new partner from an existing customer. On a tenant with no customers yet the partner create fails with Settings' message; create one customer and send again.

## Invites wait for the Business step

Like every email, the partner invite waits until DFRNT has ticked the tenant's system settings item.
