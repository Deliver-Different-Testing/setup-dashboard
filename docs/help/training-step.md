The Training step lists challenges for four roles (dispatcher, admin, accounts, driver), each worth XP. Completing challenges moves a person through five levels: Rookie from 0 XP, Operator from 100, Pro from 300, Expert from 600 and Master from 1000. Each role ends in a Master challenge that unlocks only when every other challenge in that role is done.

Progress is saved with the setup session and shown on a leaderboard for the team.

## Who appears

Everyone on the Team step, plus people added through Import on this step. Each person's role decides which challenge path they see.

## Examples of challenges

Dispatcher: First Dispatch, Shadow Mode (review auto-dispatch suggestions), Zero Touch (email orders with no manual step). Admin: First Client, Zone Creator, Data Migrator (import 50+ clients). Accounts: First Invoice, Connected Books (sync to Xero or QuickBooks). Driver: App Ready, First Delivery, POD Pro (photo + signature).

## Nothing is written to the apps

Training is tracked in the dashboard only; completing a challenge doesn't change any record in Settings or Routed Operations.
